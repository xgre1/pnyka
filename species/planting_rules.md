# Planting rules (hard constraints) for street trees in Athens

Every rule below was READ in the full document text (not from a search snippet) unless marked otherwise.
Page numbers are the printed page numbers of the PDF. Access date for all: 2026-11-21.

## 0. Important caveats

1. The City of Athens Green Regulation PDF is headed "όπως κυρώθηκε από την ΑΔΣ ……/2024" (decision number left blank in the published file). It IS ratified: City Council decision ΑΔΣ 379/26-06-2024, Diavgeia ΑΔΑ Ρ425Ω6Μ-ΤΣΒ (34 in favour, 2 abstentions); its attachment starts with the same cover and ends «Το παρόν προσαρτάται στην με αρ. 379/26-06-2024 ΑΔΣ». The repo copy `raw/species/reg.pdf` has the same SHA-256 as the live PDF. No later amendment found in Diavgeia decision titles (6/2024 to 21/11/2026). Checked 21/11/2026.
2. **The Regulation contains NO list of approved, recommended or forbidden species, NO minimum pit size for new trees, NO planting spacing, and NO distances to buildings, poles or utilities (it only refers to "the distances allowed under the applicable legislation", Art. 15 par. 5).** Art. 14 says the specifications come from "ισχύουσα Νομοθεσία" via a municipal phytotechnical study. Any such number in the app must come from the sources in sections B and C, not from the Regulation.
3. Sources disagree on the minimum sidewalk width. Regulation Art. 15: 1.50 m / 1.00 m thresholds for REPLANTING existing pits. Athens Voice 2022 (head of Green Dept): "ο νέος κτιριοδομικός κανονισμός δεν επιτρέπει τη δεντροφύτευση σε πεζοδρόμια με πλάτος μικρότερο των 2,20 μέτρων". CNN 8 Feb 2026 (head of Green Dept, Γεωργουσάκης): 1.50 m free pedestrian path, 2.20 m free height, trees only in the street-furniture zone. The primary legal text of the building/accessibility code was NOT retrieved, so the 2.20 m figure is secondary-source only. Show it to users as "reported by the City", with the quote.

Corrections to rows above: Art. 15 par.2 reopens covered pits only if covered «χωρίς ειδική αιτιολόγηση»; Art. 15 par.3 also requires a «Πρωτόκολλο κοπής/διπλής φύτευσης» and new positions are sought «με τη συνδρομή… εθελοντών»; Art. 16 also needs a decision of the competent municipal body and sets a minimum EUR 40,000 compensation for transplanting a historic tree; Art. 36: the 20 days count from completion of works, the Green Dept sets the species, and removal for danger carries no obligation for the applicant; Art. 34 refers to "άρθρο 26 παράγραφος 9", which is the Regulation's own cross-reference error (it is Art. 27 par.9).

### Where a tree does not fit (what the Regulation says)

READ result: the Regulation does NOT define alternatives to trees. It only says:
- Art. 15 par.4: pits in sidewalks up to 1.00 m are abolished, not replanted, and the lost position is compensated by two new positions elsewhere in the same Community (the "green balance"). So for narrow sidewalks the Regulation's remedy is relocation, not a substitute planting type.
- Art. 12 par.23, p.11: «Η τοποθέτηση ζαρντινιερών σε δημοτικούς χώρους πρασίνου» is a prohibited action (fine table row 23). Planters are therefore NOT an approved substitute inside municipal green spaces unless done by the municipality / permitted (par.22).
- Art. 2, p.4 lists "Παρτέρια" (beds), "Κήποι δωμάτων" (roof gardens) and "Δενδροστοιχίες ή μεμονωμένα δέντρα επί πεζοδρομίων" as municipal green categories. Art. 3 lists "Περιορισμός της ηχορύπανσης μέσω φυτικών πετασμάτων και άλλων παρεμφερών μεθόδων" as a benefit.
- Art. 12 par.5 prohibits shop awnings/structures that obstruct trees.
- Green walls, pergolas and shade structures are NOT mentioned anywhere in the Regulation (searched: πέργκολα, κατακόρυφ*, σκίαση, σκιάστρα: no hits).

Other City of Athens practice (READ, secondary):
- CNN 8 Feb 2026: landscape architect Κατσογιάννη (ΠΕΕΓΕΠ president, NOT a City official) proposes pocket parks, planting around public buildings, incentives for roofs, courtyards and climbers on walls; Γεωργουσάκης (City) calls for "αύξηση διαπερατών επιφανειών, αποκατάσταση εδαφών" and understorey planting.
- CNN 485979 (July 2025 City announcement): 400 bougainvillea and 600 myrtle planted in the Panepistimiou beds; 65 rosemary in Alsos Ilision.
- City phytotechnical study Plateia Theatrou 2020: green facade on Evripidou 45 (grasses, aromatics) and Parthenocissus tricuspidata on a building; Myrtus communis as an evergreen shrub row.

## B. City of Athens technical document: phytotechnical study, Plateia Theatrou (Dec 2020)

Source: https://www.cityofathens.gr/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/2.-20201214_fytotexnika.pdf (READ in full; signed by City of Athens officials, 14-16 Dec 2020). This is a project-specific design, not a general rule, but it is the only City-issued document with pit sizes and tree specifications that was found.

- Pit sizes, §4.1, p.10: «Η φύτευση των περισσοτέρων δένδρων θα γίνει σε λάκκους διαστάσεων 0.70Χ0.70Χ0.70μ. ενώ αυτά με μεγάλη μπάλα (Ακακία Κωνσταντινουπόλεως και Νερατζιά) ... 1.20Χ1.20Χ1.20μ. Για τη φύτευση της ελιάς ... 2.00Χ2.000Χ1.00μ. Οι θάμνοι ... 0.50Χ0.50Χ0.50μ. Σε κάθε περίπτωση ο λάκκος θα πρέπει να είναι 70% μεγαλύτερος από την μπάλα του φυτού.»
- Tree stock, p.11: «Τα δένδρα θα είναι διακλαδισμένα σε τέσσερις τουλάχιστον βραχίονες σε ύψος 2.00μ. ... περίμετρο κορμού τουλάχιστον 16εκ. σε ύψος 1.00μ. Το ύψος των δένδρων θα είναι τουλάχιστον 2.50μ.»
- Planting season, §4.3, p.11: October to March.
- Selection criteria, p.8: species of the Greek flora, drought and air-pollution tolerant, low maintenance, dense foliage and fast growth for shade.
- Climate, p.3-6 (Hellinikon station 1955-97; absolute max 42 C, absolute min -3.2 C; National Observatory 1961-90 annual rainfall 376 mm): useful for the app's climate assumptions.

## C. Greek national technical specification (ΕΛΟΤ)

Source: ΕΛΟΤ ΤΠ 1501-10-05-01-00:2009 "Φυτεύσεις δένδρων - θάμνων" (Government Gazette B, pp. 35041-35054), PDF https://sate.gr/html/pdfDocuments/10-05-01-00.pdf. The PDF text layer is font-garbled, so pages 8-9 were READ as page images (not text-extracted).

- §5.4.2, p.8-9: pit sizes are standardised as 20x20x30 cm, 30x30x30, 50x50x50, 70x70x70, 100x100x100, 120x120x120 cm and "Λάκκοι με διαστάσεις μεγαλύτερες από 120 cm". Selection criteria: «Οι διαστάσεις των λάκκων θα είναι τουλάχιστον 70% μεγαλύτερες από τη διάμετρο της μπάλας χώματος ή του φυτοδοχείου σε γαιώδη έδαφος, ή/και μέχρι 100% στα βραχώδη έδαφη.» For plants heavier than 250 kg: «η διάμετρος του λάκκου πρέπει να είναι τουλάχιστον διπλάσια από τη διάμετρο της μπάλας και το βάθος ίσο με το ύψος αυτής».
- §5.4.1: pit sides slightly inclined, bottom level and uncompacted, bottom loosened a further 5-10 cm; top 30 cm of soil kept separate.
- §5.2.3: do not plant in frozen or waterlogged or dry soil.
- This specification does NOT give sidewalk widths, tree spacing or distances to buildings/utilities. Those were not found in any national text READ in this work.

NOT used: ΕΛΟΤ ΤΠ 1501-10-05-09-00 (tree staking) was downloaded but its text layer is garbled and it was not read page by page; ΕΛΟΤ ΤΠ 1501-10-05-08-00 (transplanting) downloaded, not read. The ΕΤΕΠ site returned HTTP 404 for 1501-10-05-02-00.
