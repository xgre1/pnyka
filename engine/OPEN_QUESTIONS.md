# Open questions: judgement calls for the humans

Each item is a choice made in this reference calculation. Change it in the file named.

1. **+1 percentage point as the tree quantity** (`params.csv` trees_pavement.canopy_increment_pp_package). It follows the +1/+3/+5 scenarios of `research/cost/scenarios.md`; +3 and +5 appear as options. Other choices change every tree package and the budget results.
2. **10% as the canopy target** (`_global.canopy_target_pct`). Defines eligibility and capacity. The capacity file also offers 5, 15, 30.
3. **Crown 35 m2 and survival 50% as central.** 35 m2 equals the published 20-year LARGE-tree value (small 18.7, medium 23.6), so central is optimistic against 20-year data. Survival is a scenario choice between 35% and 79%, not measured in Athens.
4. **Low/high of costs and quantities combine all favourable or all unfavourable parameters.** It is a scenario range, not a statistical interval.
5. **50/50 mix of existing and new small pits** (`share_new_small_pits`), and exclusion of the 10-15 m3 root volume option (3b, international prices, 3,367-10,104 EUR). Including 3b would multiply tree costs by about 13.
6. **Score formula.** RESOLVED for trees and roofs on 2026-11-21 as the audit recommended (`validation/VERDICT.md`): trees = need score re-ranked to a percentile among the tree packages, then half need and half people (balanced, change C); roofs = mean of the tree-cover and surface-temperature percentiles. STILL OPEN: the weights are a choice, not a measurement (trees 13.9 of the top 20 stay under random weights, roofs 12.8): the weight between need and people (trees) and between tree cover and surface temperature (roofs) should be stated as policy. The other three interventions keep the additive mean; whether to balance them the same way was not audited.
7. **Percentiles are computed within each intervention for the people part**, so scores of different interventions are only an ordering aid; the top-20 list mixes them. A per-intervention ranking is also printed.
8. **Which indicators count as need for each intervention** (rules.csv). RESOLVED for trees (tree cover, new trees per 1000 residents) and roofs (tree cover, satellite surface temperature) by the audit. Open for the others: school street has only population density because no traffic or NO2 data exist per neighbourhood; cool surface and elderly still use night temperature (sensor-only) and were not audited. The population that equals the people basis is left out to avoid double counting.
9. **People concerned**: all residents for trees, children 0-14 for school streets and cool surfaces, residents 65+ for elderly support, none for roofs. These populations differ, so people per euro is not comparable across interventions: trees (all residents, cheap) dominate budget mode.
10. **Packages are whole-neighbourhood and indivisible** in budget mode; no partial packages, no limit of one intervention per neighbourhood. A neighbourhood can receive trees and school streets that reach the same people.
11. **Elderly eligibility** requires a positive canopy deficit (heat-exposure gap proxy). The impact ceiling assumes 100% coverage and uses a municipal heat-death baseline that is an assumption (FUNCTION.md). Should neighbourhood-level death numbers be shown at all, given +-25-30% population error?
12. **School street = one scheme per school** (upper bound), priced with London FOI costs (20,700 / 64,800 / 172,500 EUR). No Athens price exists.
13. **Cool surface packages have no cost** until the City gives a yard area (or default). Roofs now have a cost from an ASSUMED price (item 19); elderly support still has none. Decide whether to ask the mentor or let the user type the numbers in the app.
14. **Roof quantity uses matched school + municipal building footprints** (lower bound, one building per point). The upper bound in the capacity summary is 3.1x for schools and 5.1x for other municipal buildings.
15. **New-tree counts** come from `athenstrees/plantings_by_neighbourhood.csv`; the capacity file has its own count (`plantings_since_2024_trees`). They can differ slightly; survival is unknown, possible duplicate records were not removed.
16. **Night temperature only exists where a sensor exists** and is left missing elsewhere. RESOLVED for trees and roofs: it is no longer in their score (it stays an out-of-sample check). Still open for cool surface and elderly, where a missing value means those packages are scored on fewer indicators.
17. **Maintenance cost (23.5 EUR/tree/year)** is shown but not in the cost or the budget. Should the budget include 20 years of maintenance as in the scenarios (it roughly doubles the cost)?
18. **Name join** between files is by neighbourhood name (Unicode NFC, whitespace-normalised). Unmatched names are printed to stderr and stay missing.
19. **Roof unit cost 13.5 / 20 / 25 EUR/m2** (`params.csv` roof_cool_or_green_municipal.roof_cost_per_m2_eur, status assumption). Low is a 2012 City of Athens price for a waterproofing membrane (not a cool coating, not inflation-adjusted); central and high are a vendor web page (snippet, no method). Low confidence; the City should overwrite it with a quote. It excludes roof preparation. The cost is computed on the whole matched footprint, so it is an upper bound of area (usable flat share unknown) and a lower bound of buildings (not all municipal buildings matched). Decide whether roof costs may be shown to the public with this price, and whether the green roof price (45 / 110 / 320 EUR/m2 in the defaults file) should be offered as an option.
20. **Budget order.** RESOLVED by the team decision σημείωμα ομάδας: one budget per tab (trees, roofs), spent in `rank_within_intervention` order; that is the default of `--budget` and of `budget_250k/1m/5m.md`. `--rank-by people_per_eur` stays as a non-default comparison; `--rank-by need` and `budget_1m_by_need.md` were removed. Still open: whether school streets (priced, London prices) should get a tab or a budget; the decision covers two tabs only.
21. **People concerned for roofs is still empty.** `sites_n` (matched buildings) is available, but occupants per building are unknown. Whether to estimate people for roofs, and how the people-concerned figure or the score should use `sites_n`, is left open on purpose: nothing in the score or in `people_concerned` changed.
22. **`rank_within_intervention` follows `priority_score`**, so it inherits open questions 6 to 9 (score formula, percentiles within the intervention, need indicators, people basis). Ties at the 4 decimals shown in the csv: roofs by matched roof area, larger first, then name (13 of the 22 tied roof pairs are ordered differently from the alphabetical order of the audit); all others by neighbourhood name. The tree list still has 15 groups of tied scores (32 packages) ordered by name: should trees break ties by residents too?
23. **`sites_n` definitions**: roofs count unique matched school and other municipal building polygons (one facility can be several buildings); elderly counts clubs (Lesches Filias), not residents; trees count all four site types with canopy below 5% within 150 m, so it does not say how many sites a tree package actually shades.
24. **Greek labels (`labels_el.csv`) are a first draft** for a municipal reader. They need a read-through by a Greek speaker of the team before they go on screen, in particular the impact labels (they must keep the meaning of up to) and the gap labels.
25. **Codes in `packages.json`** (data gaps, evidence, people basis, source codes) are new vocabulary chosen here. They are stable names: renaming one breaks the labels file and the screen.
26. **`engine/validation/validation.py` audited the OLD formula** and stops (reproduction check) if run on the new engine. Its numbers for the new formulas are those of its variants `TC_balanced_cover_newtrees_1000` and `R6_tree_cover_plus_lst`, which the engine now reproduces (tested). Should the audit be re-run with the new formula as baseline (it would also need `surface_temp_lst_c` in its indicator table)?
27. **The `sources` column of the roof packages does not name `treecover/treecover_by_neighbourhood.csv`**, where both roof indicators come from (tree cover already did before). It was left unchanged because `sources` is frozen by the contract of this change; adding the file to `rules.csv` `data_sources` of the roof would change that column for 115 rows.
28. **Satellite surface temperature** (`surface_temp_lst_c`): City of Athens GIS layer (EXTREMA / ARTi), 2016-2020, licence not stated in `treecover/metrics_dictionary.csv`. It is surface, not air, temperature; it flags hot bare hills (ΦΙΛΟΠΑΠΠΟΥ is rank 11 of the roofs) and it is now inside the roof score, so a check of the roof list against LST is circular. Check the licence before showing it.
29. **Text outside `engine/` still describes the old ranking**: `engine/compare/build_compare.py` labels the need side "composite score, PROVISIONAL" with caveat `composite_score_provisional_pending_audit`; `landing/data.js`, `app/public/data/` and `mockup/data.js` were built from the old `packages.json` / compare output and need a rebuild by their owners.
