# What a budget of 5,000,000 EUR buys in each tab (reference calculation)

Default mode (`--rank-by per_tab`), following the team decision σημείωμα ομάδας: each tab has its OWN budget of 5,000,000 EUR. The two tabs are not added together and not compared with each other: there is no common unit of impact. Rule: the packages of the intervention that have a central cost are walked in `rank_within_intervention` order (1 first, the same order as the colour of the map). A package is indivisible: it is taken if its central cost fits in what is left, otherwise it is skipped and the next one is tried, down to the end of the list. Greedy, not optimal, deterministic. Costs are central values; low-high is the scenario range of the same selection.
